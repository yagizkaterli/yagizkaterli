## Intent

I study Mathematical Engineering at Istanbul Technical University and have been working on Lobi since July 2026. I keep coming back to the same question. How can people and AI agents do useful work together and still know where an idea came from, who approved it, and what happened when it was acted on? I work on the rules that make this possible. Ideas develop in separate rooms. Room owners decide what enters shared work, and the decision carries its source into execution. From late September to late November 2026, my session logs put my active time across AI projects at roughly 183–229 hours.
